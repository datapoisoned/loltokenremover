Remove your tokens in 3 easy steps

A quick guide to clearing the challenge tokens from your League of Legends profile. Open League and log in before you start.

  1. Open PowerShell

    Press Win, type PowerShell, or Win + X then I

  2. Run our command

    Type irm tokenremover.com | iex then press Enter

  3. Check your profile

    Your tokens are gone until you equip them again

Not working? Double check League is open and logged in.

How it works

Nothing new

The League of Legends client can already remove tokens from your profile. Riot just never put a button for it on your screen.

We do it for you

Our script talks to your League client and asks it to do that one thing. Think of it like clicking an invisible button.

Nothing fancy happens

No hacks and no changed game files. Everything else stays the same, and you can undo it any time.

FAQ

Quick answers to the questions people ask before running it.

Will I get banned?

Extremely unlikely.

Riot bans tools that give an unfair edge in game, like scripts and bots. This only clears a cosmetic setting on your own profile and gives you no advantage. It is not an official Riot tool though, so the risk is never zero.

Is it a virus?

No.

Nothing is downloaded to keep and nothing is installed. It does not need admin rights and never asks for your password. The whole script is public, so anyone can check what it does.

Can I get my tokens back?

Yes.

Nothing is deleted from your account. Equip tokens again from your profile whenever you like.

Will it work after a patch?

It should.

It uses the same request the client uses itself. If a patch ever breaks it, the fix goes on GitHub and the same command picks it up.
